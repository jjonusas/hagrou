### What should a candidate for the Hagrou adventure know about you?

Sometimes reserved, sometimes assertive, I enjoy spending time with my loved
ones and learning from others. I’m a psychology researcher; I love crafts,
music, watching films and documentaries, going for walks and soaking up the
sun. Gentleness and a critical mind form the fabric of who I am.

### Why did you choose to live in a co-housing community (with Hagrou)?

Living in a habitat groupé has been a dream of mine for several years now. It
combines my desire to live in a community with my need for stability. My dream:
a warm and welcoming home. In Hagrou, I find values that are dear to me and the
conviction that I will grow thanks to the group and the project.
