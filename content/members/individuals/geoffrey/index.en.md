### What should a candidate for the Hagrou adventure know about you?

A cheerful thirty-something, I love making my friends and family laugh.

A weekend sports enthusiast who enjoys both getting away from it all and
literary exploration, I love spending time with people over
a beer in a pub.

A video editor by trade, playing video games, role-playing  or watching films
are some of my little pleasures. I’m very curious by nature and love organising
and taking part in all sorts of activities.

An extrovert with a slightly shy streak, Team ENM, feminist, ally and LGBTQIA+
friendly, ️I also share a flat with my cat Mike.

### Why did you choose to live in a co-housing community (with Hagrou)?

My main motivations for the project are to live with people who share my values
and hobbies, in a welcoming environment with green spaces.
