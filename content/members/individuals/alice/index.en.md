### What should a candidate for the Hagrou adventure know about you?

An introvert who loves words that reflect the world; I listen to them and play
with them passionately… before recharging my batteries by reading in the garden
in a pink sequinned skirt, (poorly) hidden behind large sunglasses.

### Why did you choose to live in a co-housing community (with Hagrou)?

I’d be quite tempted to go and hide away in the forest, near a river where I
could swim and among rocks from which to gain a bit of height, but I’d soon
feel melancholic and lonely there. Brussels allows me to stay connected in
various ways to a humanity that I find beautiful, because it is full of
creativity and hope. I dream that Hagrou, whilst broadening my social horizons,
will weave even more tightly those bonds of affection that nurture the capacity
for self-reflection and resilience.
