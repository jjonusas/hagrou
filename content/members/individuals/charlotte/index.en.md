### What should a candidate for the Hagrou adventure know about you?

I’m Tiago’s foster mum, in love with Florent, and a French teacher; I love
spending time with my family and friends. I also enjoy reading, going to
concerts, watching films and plays, going for walks, cycling, soaking up the
sun, strolling around Brussels, and discovering a new beer or a new park (this
list isn’t exhaustive).

### Why did you choose to join a housing collective (with Hagrou)?

A housing collective to rethink the way we live (in our homes and in a broader
sense) and to consider alternatives to the paths imposed by capitalism:
sharing, solidarity, inclusivity and openness.
