### What should a candidate for the Hagrou adventure know about you?

Sunday 26 April 2026, 2.03 pm: In 27 minutes, I have to set off for the Hagrou
meeting. Settled comfortably on my yellow sofa, I’m waiting for a fascinating
fun fact about myself to surface in my mind. A gentle breeze caresses my cheek.
My eyelids feel heavy and the soft purr of engines below lulls me. My eyes are
closing… Come on, just a few minutes…

### Why did you choose communal living (with Hagrou)?

I’m here for the pond, the hens, and the vegetable patch! More seriously, the
three pillars of our project (robustness, brotherhood and poetry), along with
the proposals that accompany them, are, for me, equally enticing responses to
the cynical individualism in which it’s all too easy to get bogged down.
