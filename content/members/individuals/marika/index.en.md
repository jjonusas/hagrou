### What should a candidate for the Hagrou adventure know about you?

After two years in Brussels, where I joined Hagrou, I’ve been in Montpellier
since mid-2026, where I continue to be part of the adventure remotely. I enjoy
cultural outings, board games, travelling by train to visit my friends, I do
dance classes and my goal this year is to keep my plants alive!

### Why did you choose to live in a housing collective (with Hagrou)?

After living for many years in shared flats or as part of a couple, I finally
had to face the facts: I love living with people I get on with, but I also need
my own space and my independence. Collective living therefore emerged as the
perfect solution, and when I came across Hagrou, I recognised myself in all its
values: sharing, solidarity, kindness, environmental awareness, creativity,
playfulness, and so much more!
