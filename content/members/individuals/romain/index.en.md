### What should a candidate for the Hagrou adventure know about you?

It was a series of happy coincidences that brought me to Belgium and then into
this group, and I’m delighted to be building these relationships with my future
neighbours. I’m fascinated by everything to do with games and group dynamics. I
win and lose arguments – not particularly important ones, mind you – but I do
so with conviction when it comes to the names of the working groups within
Hagrou.

### Why did you choose collective housing (with Hagrou)?

For me, collective housing strikes the right balance between living alone and
sharing a flat. I need a home where I can relax and also build strong
relationships with my neighbours, creating a community.
