### Why did you choose to live in a co-housing community (with Hagrou)?

Living close to my loved ones and outside the prescribed, isolating structures
of society are desires that have driven me for a long time. The opportunity to
join a creative community of lovely people came along, and I seized it! If I
live with my friends, I won’t need to travel to go and see them, hehe.
